What to judge

  • At 24px, every Morph and Gradient avatar shows two eyes and a mouth, not a smudge.
  • No face looks creepy or sad by accident. Name any seed that does.
  • Faces sit in the body of the shape, never on a thin lobe or touching an edge.
  • The two-dot catch-light reads as one recognizable Opaline eye.
  • Aura feels like milky glass, not a copy of a marble or sunset gradient.
  • Each palette works on both white and near-black.
